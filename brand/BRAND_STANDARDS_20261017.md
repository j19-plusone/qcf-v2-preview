## 1. Master Foundation Emblem · 基金会官方标识
**Asset:** `brand/qcf-official-logo.svg`
**Identity:** Original intertwined metallic gold Q and graphite/silver-black QCF letterform, supplied and approved by the founder. This is the primary QCF institution mark.
**Authorized uses:** Website navigation/header and footer, official foundation documents, founding charter, institutional reports, presentation covers, partner materials, press kits, foundation-branded posters.
**Do not:** Replace with the old circular Q icon, Civilization ID fingerprint, or contribution Q; distort geometry; recolor into flat yellow; invent a new letterform; use a misleading UN emblem.
**Technical note:** Current SVG wraps an embedded raster source with a near-white transparency filter. It is NOT a true vector redraw. Retain the founder's original image as master source. A manually verified transparent PNG / professionally traced vector master may be added later without altering the mark.
**Presentation:** Preserve metallic gold and graphite contrast; use transparent-background version against deep-navy/black; maintain clear space of at least 20% of emblem width and avoid overlaying the Earth subject.

## 2. Civilization ID Mark · 文明 ID 标识
**Asset:** `brand/qcf-civ-id-mark.svg`
**Identity:** Gold fingerprint-based identity symbol, visually distinct from the foundation emblem.
**Authorized uses:** Civilization ID issuance/verification screens, personal ID credentials, user identity records, secure identity experiences, verified contribution attribution.
**Do not:** Use as QCF foundation logo or contribution-unit symbol.
**Privacy and security:** Symbol is an illustration, not proof of biometric authentication. Do not expose or encode real fingerprint data in the logo. An actual ID system requires explicit consent, security review, and a privacy-preserving design.

## 3. Q Contribution Symbol · 量子小 Q 贡献值符号
**Asset:** `brand/qcf-q-glyph.svg`
**Identity:** Small gold Q symbol representing non-transferable contribution points in the proposed QCF co-building ecosystem.
**Authorized uses:** Contribution points counters, verified contribution records, non-financial recognition, internal incentive dashboards.
**Do not:** Use as foundation institution logo, Civilization ID emblem, cryptocurrency logo, settlement currency, or promise of investment return.
**Compliance:** Q contribution points are not represented as cash, securities, tokenized assets, or exchangeable financial value. Any future benefit mechanism requires independent legal and regulatory assessment.

## 6. Change Control · 版本与审查
- Master repo remains stable; date-based version record: `QCF-Website YYYYMMDD`; additional updates: `-V2`, `-V3`.
- Every identity change requires: change summary, source asset, approval, commit SHA, deployment status and live verification.
- Never silently replace or reinterpret one of the three marks.
- QCF remains a Swiss foundation project in preparation; avoid implying UN affiliation or registration until formally confirmed.
