When life ends, does our digital world disappear — or live on?

One Human. One Civilization ID. One Digital Legacy.

Every day we log in. To our phones, email, banks, health services, government portals and cloud accounts. Across borders, we repeat passwords, codes and identity checks. Our lives are fragmented across hundreds of digital doors.

We spend a lifetime creating digital assets, footprints, knowledge, memories and businesses. If we grow old, lose capacity, or pass away, will our loved ones even know what exists? Should all that we built simply vanish?

Your identity. Your sovereignty. Your legacy.

QCF proposes Civilization ID × NU-Key: an open research vision for self-controlled digital identity, verifiable credentials, secure access, an inventory of digital assets, and lawful digital inheritance. A universal key is not a master password that bypasses every platform. It is a user-controlled gateway that coordinates separate credentials and permissions.

From first login to a lasting legacy

Protect privacy while growing up. Navigate identity across borders. Govern business and AI-agent access. Prepare trusted delegates as we age. Preserve, delete or pass on selected digital assets according to our wishes and applicable law.

Life has an end. Civilization must go on.

Loss of consciousness is not death. A breathing signal or biometric event must never independently trigger irreversible asset transfer. Emergency delegation and inheritance require separate safeguards, documented consent, independent verification and legal review. Civilization ID does not replace passports, national e-ID or bank KYC.
