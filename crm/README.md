# QCF Foundation CRM

**QCF | Co-creating the Next Civilization**

Open-source foundation CRM and governance operations system for foundations, charities, associations, NGOs, research institutions and public-interest organisations.

## Core modules

1. Civilization Co-builders / Civilization ID
2. Interest subscriptions and bilingual research briefs
3. Donations and donor records
4. Public-interest activities and event registration
5. Civilization voting
6. Civilization initiatives / calls to action
7. News flash distribution
8. Communication campaigns and delivery logs
9. Consent, unsubscribe, suppression and privacy controls
10. Governance audit log and role-based administration

## Foundation-stage policy

During the QCF preparation stage, QCF research briefs are free for registered Civilization Co-builders.

Planned sender: **Founder@qcf.org.cn**.

The qcf.org.cn domain has been created in Resend but automated sending must remain disabled until DNS verification succeeds.

## Reuse from Neumann

The architecture reuses proven patterns already present in the Neumann website backend: subscriber lifecycle, consent state, confirmation-token workflow, Resend delivery, unsubscribe, rate limiting and audit-oriented operations.

QCF extends these patterns into a foundation-specific CRM rather than copying business-only workflows.

## Open-source strategy

Working generic name: **Foundation Commons CRM**.

QCF is the reference implementation. The reusable core should support foundations, charities, NGOs, associations, research institutes and community organisations without requiring QCF branding.
