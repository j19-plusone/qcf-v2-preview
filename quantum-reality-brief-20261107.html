Rethinking Humanity: Quantum Physics Is Redefining Our Understanding of Reality

Jessica Sage · Civilisation Architect · QCF Founder

Is the world we perceive the world as it really is? In a feature published on 6 November 2026, Charlie Wood of Quanta Magazine revisits a century of debate over quantum theory and physical reality.

Three milestones

1935 — EPR: Einstein, Podolsky and Rosen questioned whether quantum mechanics offers a complete description of physical reality.

1964 — Bell: Bell derived a testable constraint on local hidden-variable accounts. Subsequent experiments showed that quantum correlations violate those classical constraints.

2012 — PBR: Pusey, Barrett and Rudolph showed that, under specified assumptions, the quantum state cannot be merely incomplete knowledge of an underlying physical state. The theorem does not prove that objective reality is nonexistent or that consciousness creates reality.

Jessica Sage's perspective

Progress begins by challenging assumptions. The history of science is not a catalogue of permanent certainties but an ongoing process of revising models in response to evidence.

Future civilization needs self-correcting knowledge systems. As AI, quantum technologies and life sciences advance, scientific claims must remain open to verification, criticism and revision.

Wisdom includes recognizing the limits of knowledge. A model can predict experiments with extraordinary accuracy without resolving every philosophical question about reality.

A QCF principle

QCF advocates open, evidence-based and continuously revisable knowledge governance. We should not treat today's understanding as the endpoint of civilization, but as the beginning of our next inquiry.

思考人类：量子力学正在重新定义我们对现实的理解

文明架构师 Jessica Sage · QCF 发起人

我们看到的世界，是否就是世界真实的样子？2026年11月6日，Quanta Magazine记者Charlie Wood发表深度报道，回顾量子理论与物理现实之间延续一个世纪的争论。

三个科学里程碑

1935年——EPR：爱因斯坦、Podolsky与Rosen质疑量子力学是否完整描述物理现实。

1964年——贝尔定理：贝尔提出可检验的局域隐变量约束；后续实验显示量子关联违反相关经典约束。

2012年——PBR定理：在明确假设下，量子态不能仅被解释为对隐藏物理状态的不完整知识。该定理没有证明客观现实不存在，也没有证明意识创造现实。

文明架构师 Jessica Sage 的观点

文明进步始于挑战既有认知。 科学并不是永久正确的结论集合，而是通过证据不断修正模型的过程。

未来文明需要可自我修正的知识体系。 AI、量子科技与生命科学越发展，我们越需要可验证、可质疑、可修正的知识治理机制。

真正的智慧包括理解知识的边界。 模型即使能精确预测实验，也不代表有关现实本质的哲学争论已经结束。
