# QCF editorial policy

Confirmed by Jessica Sage on 5 November 2026 (Europe/Zurich).

- Future QCF news and research briefs use the byline **文明架构师 Jessica Sage**; English: **Civilisation Architect Jessica Sage**. This refers to QCF editorial authorship, not authorship of the underlying research. Preserve explicit third-party author credits.
- Publish at least one life-sciences report each week. Default editorial slot: Thursday morning, Europe/Zurich.
- Use the existing production repository `j19-plusone/qcf-v2-preview`, branch `main`, CNAME `qcf.org.cn`; publication runs through existing GitHub Pages. Verify this configuration before future changes. Do not publish to the older `qcf-website` repository.
- Research from primary sources. Distinguish publication dates, research findings, hypotheses and QCF commentary. Cite original links and DOI records. Write an original concise summary; do not reproduce paywalled articles or present a QCF byline as the original source author's.
- Publish edited English and Chinese. Default to English for new visitors and honour an explicit language choice.
- Give every report a stable second-level article URL. Add it to `civilization-briefs.html`, the `life-sciences.html#life-sciences-news` section and the homepage's compact publication list. Keep dates newest first and older articles accessible.
- Check for duplicates, preserve concurrent changes, validate HTML and links, then confirm deployment and article visibility. If blocked, report the actual blocker and preserve a ready draft; do not claim publication.
